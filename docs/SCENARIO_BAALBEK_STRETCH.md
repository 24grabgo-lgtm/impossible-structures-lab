# Scenario — Baalbek Stretch (v0.2)

## Goal
Move and seat **one** 800–1200 t-class block onto a platform line. No full temple.

## Why after trilithon
Same systems; mass is the teacher. Geometry simpler; logistics harder.

## Map
Quarry ledge → short prepared way → platform bed.

## Default reality check
Baseline Neolithic flags alone may be **insufficient** at true mass — that is a feature. Player must:
- Scale labor abstraction, or
- Enable classical machines, or
- Enable speculative flags and own it on the report

## Win
Block on bed, level within tolerance, no catastrophic tip, report exported.

## Non-goal
Micron joint debates as required win — optional precision modifier only.
