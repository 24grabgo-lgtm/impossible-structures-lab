# Quarry & Dressing (pre-lift)

Lifting only matters if the stone exists and fits.

## Extract (experimental / ethnographic toolkit)

| Method | Idea | Risk |
|--------|------|------|
| Channel + wedges | Cut slots; drive wedges (wood/water swell or hard) | Slow; controlled |
| Pounders | Bash channels with harder stone | Labor heavy |
| Fire-setting | Heat face, quench, spall | Unpredictable fracture |
| Natural joints | Exploit bedding | May force wrong blank size |
| Abrasive sawing analogue | Sand + copper/wood bow ideas (later tech flag) | Time sink; precision gain |

## Dress

- Peck → grind → polish progression  
- **Flatness** costs time; feeds lintel seat and “precision mode”  
- Tenon/mortice on trilithon: simplified mini-game or time+skill check in v0
