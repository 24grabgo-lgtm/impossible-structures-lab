| Sled on tallow-class | — | ~0.1 |

## Haul force (rule of thumb)
`F ≈ μ × mass × g` on level ground; add `m g sinθ` on grade.

Example: 40 t, μ=0.2 → ~80 kN level ≈ **8 tonnes-force** order pull before mechanical advantage.

## Sim meters
- **Labor pool** — discrete workers assigned to haul/raise/crib
- **Pull efficiency** — reduced by bad rope angle (reward A-frame)
- **Fatigue** — long pulls lose force
- **Timber stock** — crib/ramp consume units

## Tuning law
Baseline methods must be *possible but tight*. Speculative flags make it easy — and mark the report.
