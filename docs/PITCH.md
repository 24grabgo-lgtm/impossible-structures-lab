# Pitch — Impossible Structures Lab

## One line
Can you actually build it — with every method humans can think of on the table?

## Paragraph
Impossible Structures Lab is a construction-systems sandbox inspired by Kerbal-style trial-and-error and by the live debate around megalithic precision and logistics (BAM’s measurement-led questions, Hancock’s “how did they do that?” framing, and mainstream experimental archaeology). You source stone, work it, move it, and raise a target structure under honest constraints. Enable only period tools — or unlock speculative flags — and publish the method mix that finally seats the lintel.

## Why it shares DNA with the prior project
- Rules are the gameplay
- Failures teach
- Finite loadouts of methods/tools
- Sandbox vs constrained scenarios
- Shareable “I did the thing” moments

## Audience
Engineering curious players, archaeology enthusiasts across the mainstream↔alternative spectrum, educators, and anyone who liked KSP’s “one more try after the boom.”

## Fairness note
The lab does **not** require belief in a lost Ice Age civilization to be fun. It requires curiosity about **materials, mass, and method**.
